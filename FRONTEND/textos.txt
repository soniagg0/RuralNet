



Nowadays, with new technological breakthroughs, society has developed new ways to communicate and share information. Thanks to these advances, many crucial fields — such as healthcare — have started to use technology to help improve lives around the world.

We are third-year university students with a strong interest in technology, telecommunications, and development. During this project, we have created a system designed to detect medical problems in remote environments. Not only was the system engineered, but an app was also developed to track the data emerging from the main sensor.

Based on three main components — LoRa Gateway, Raspberry Pi, and Arduino — this project has made it possible to monitor and analyze health data efficiently and reliably.

We are very pleased to share this project with the world and to help others! :)
